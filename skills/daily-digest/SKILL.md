---
name: daily-digest
description: Build a short morning digest of what needs the user's attention today. Use when asked for a morning brief, daily digest, "what's on today", or when a scheduled routine asks for one.
---

Use only the sources you have tools for; skip a section silently when its tool is not connected.
Each section is at most five bullets, most important first.

1. **Needs attention:** ask the knowledge base (`aikb_search`) "what is currently broken?" and
   "what needs attention or is pending?". List open incidents, expiring certificates and pending
   items with their dates.
2. **Today:** calendar events for today with times, and anything that needs preparation.
3. **Code:** pull requests waiting on the user's review or with failing checks, and new issues
   assigned to them, across their GitHub repositories.
4. **Mail:** unread messages from real people (not newsletters or notifications) that look like
   they need a reply. Subject and sender only; never send or archive anything.
5. **One next action:** end with the single most useful thing to do first, small enough to
   start in two minutes.

Start with the most urgent item. No greeting, no recap at the end. If every source is quiet, say
so in one line.
